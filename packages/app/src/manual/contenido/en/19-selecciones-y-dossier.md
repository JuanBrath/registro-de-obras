# Selections and dossier

Build groups of hand-picked artworks and use them to generate a PDF dossier (cover, biography, text, artworks with their captions and contact details), which is saved and numbered.

## What a selection is

A **selection** is a group of artworks you pick by hand, for example the ones you will take to a fair or show to a gallery. It is useful when filters are not enough: a filter may show you ten photos and you only want five. An artwork can be in several selections at once, and removing it from a selection does not delete it. Selections have an order, which is the one the dossier follows.

## Create a selection

1. In **Artworks**, click **Selections**.
2. Click **Create selection**.
3. Type the **Selection name** and, if you want, the **Notes**. Below, **all** your artworks are shown, without filters, each with a checkbox: tick the ones you want to include (above you see how many you have chosen).
4. To see them better, switch between **Columns**, **Cards** and **Images** and change the **Thumbnail size** (the control is at the end of the bar). With **Full screen**, or with the **⤢** button on each artwork, every photo opens large, one at a time: move to the previous or next with the arrows (on screen or on the keyboard) and choose or remove it with **Choose this artwork** (or with the space bar). When you close it, what you chose stays ticked.
5. Click **Save**. The selection stays open, already with those artworks (see **View and sort a selection**). If you want to build it from a filter, do it from **Artworks** (see below).

## Groups of selections

At the top of the screen there are two buttons: **Groups** and **Selections**. **Selections** shows the list and lets you create selections. **Groups** shows only the groups you created; a **group** gathers the selections on the same theme (for example "Fairs 2026" or "Landscapes").

1. Under **Groups** click **+ New group**, type the name, click the selections that make it up (each one is a button that turns blue with ✓ when it is part of the group; if it is already in another group, it says so under the name) and click **Save**.
2. Click a group to see the selections in it and choose one. With **Add selections to the group** you add or remove selections by clicking them (saved at once), change the name or click **Delete group** (the selections are not deleted, they just have no group).
3. You can also assign the group from a selection: open it, click **Edit details**, choose its **Group** in the drop-down (or "No group") and click **Save**. Each selection belongs to a single group.

The list of selections shows only two rows; if there are more, click **Show more** to unfold them all. When you choose one, the list makes way for the open selection.

## Add artworks to a selection

There are two ways. The most convenient for many artworks is from **Artworks**:

1. Click **Select**. A panel with the **Selection** drop-down appears.
2. Choose an existing selection or **+ New selection…** (type the name and click **Create**).
3. Tick the artworks you want to add, in cards or in columns (you can use the filters to find them). Above, clearly visible, you see how many you have chosen. The ones already in the selection appear marked and cannot be ticked again.
4. Click **Add selected**. The artworks are added to the selection. When you are done, click **Done selecting**.

The other way is from an artwork's sheet: the **Add to selection…** button lets you choose an existing selection or create a new one. See [Artwork sheet](cap:ficha-de-obra).

## View and sort a selection

In **Selections**, click a selection's name to open it. The list makes way for that selection alone; **← All selections** takes you back (if you came from a group, it says **← Back to group** and its name). From top to bottom you see:

- **The summary:** the name, the number of artworks, the group and the first lines of the notes. To change the name, group or notes click **Edit details** and then **Save** (or **Cancel** to leave everything as it was). **Delete selection** is there too.
- **With this selection you can create:** **Generate PDF dossier**, **Saved dossiers**, **Export photos** and **Generate selection reports**.
- **The artworks.** You see its artworks in the same format as in [Artworks](cap:obras): you can switch between **Columns**, **Cards** and **Images** (only the photos, with the same criterion as the artwork gallery: clicking one opens it enlarged, with arrows to move on) and change the **Thumbnail size**. The star ratings can also be changed from here.

To change the order or take artworks out, in the **Columns** or **Cards** view click **Sort and remove**. While the mode is on you can drag an artwork to its new position or use the arrows, and **Remove** takes the artwork out of the selection (it does not delete it); also, clicking an artwork does not open its sheet, so it does not open by mistake while dragging. When you are done, click **Done**. Outside that mode, clicking an artwork opens its sheet, and closing it takes you back to that same selection.

## Reports for a selection

In a selection, **Generate selection reports** directly opens the reports window, with only the artworks of that selection. In **Artworks** there is also a **Selection** filter to limit the list (and the reports) to one selection. See [Reports and PDF documents](cap:informes).

## Export a selection's photos

**Export photos** exports the photos of the whole selection at once, using the same criteria as **Export image** on an artwork's sheet: it always starts from each artwork's original file.

1. Open the selection and click **Export photos**.
2. Choose the **Format** (**JPG**, **TIFF** or **Original**), the **Color depth** (TIFF only), the **Resize** checkbox (unticked, the photos come out at their original size; ticked, you choose reference side, unit —pixels or centimeters, with its resolution in ppi— and size) and the copyright. By default the copyright is automatic: "© artist name and year" of each artwork; if you untick it, you type your own for all of them.
3. Click **Choose folder and export…** and choose the folder where the photos are saved.

Each photo is saved with the artwork's title as its name, and never overwrites a file that is already in the folder (if the name exists, it adds `_2`, `_3`…). Artworks without an original file loaded cannot be exported: when it finishes, the program tells you which ones were left out.

## Generate a dossier

The window has four tabs: **1. Pages**, **2. Cover**, **3. Texts** and **4. Captions and closing**. Move between them by clicking them, or with **Next** to go through them in order; nothing you entered is lost. **Generate PDF** is always at the bottom, so you do not need to visit them all if what is already chosen works for you. At the top right, the **?** button offers the **Visual tour** of the window (it opens each tab by itself and returns to the one you were on) and **Search the manual**. **Esc** closes the help first; the dossier stays open with what you entered.

1. Open the selection and click **Generate PDF dossier**.
2. In the **Pages** tab, under **Page color** choose **White**, **Neutral**, **Cool** or **Warm** (pastel shades) and set the **Intensity** with the slider; next to it you see a sample of the page. The color applies to every page of the dossier and the program remembers the last one you used.
3. Under **Format** choose the **Page size** (A4 or Legal (Oficio)), the **Orientation**, the **Language** (**Español**, **English** or **Spanish and English (both)**) and the **Works per page**. If you open **Pages that differ from the rest**, with **+ Add a different page (number or color)** you can make a specific artwork page carry more or fewer artworks and, with **Color of this page**, give it its own color and intensity (**Same as the rest** uses the dossier color).
4. In the **Cover** tab choose the **Cover text** (by default the artist's name), whether to add the selection name, an **Additional text (optional)**, the **Typeface** (**Modern**, **Geometric**, **Classic** or **Typewriter**, with a sample below), whether it goes in **Italic** (works with all four) and the **Font size (pt)**. The typeface applies to the cover texts and the program remembers the last one you used.
5. In the **Texts** tab review the **Artist biography** and the **Statement**. Both are editable, apply only to this dossier and have the **View full** button. The statement starts empty; if you leave it empty, that page is not included. If the biography has lines that **start** with a year or a period (for example "2019 – Solo show" or "2015-present: teacher"), the **Show the years in the biography as a timeline** checkbox appears, already ticked: in the PDF the dates come out on one side, joined by a line, with what happened next to each. At least two dates in a row are needed; the order you wrote them in is kept and the rest comes out as text. You can also write the year alone on a line and, below it, one line for each thing that happened that year.
6. In the **Captions and closing** tab, under **Caption for each work**, tick what you want to show: **Artist name in each caption**, **Title and year**, **Technique**, **Dimensions**, **Price**, **Edition number** and **Artist's proofs**. For the price you can edit the text that goes **After the price** (for example "+ VAT") and the one for **When several works share a caption** ("each").
7. In the same tab, under **Closing page**, choose whether the **Contact at the end** is included.
8. Click **Generate PDF**. The dossier is saved automatically.

> For each artwork, the data comes from its single copy or, if it is an edition, from the first copy that is available, in stock, on exhibition, on consignment or in production. Sold, reserved, discarded, destroyed and artist's-collection copies are ignored.

> In Recer Studio the artist's name is not repeated in each caption (it is already on the cover); in Recer Space it is. You can still change it with the **Artist name in each caption** checkbox.

With **Spanish and English (both)**, the biography and the statement have one field for each language and come out on separate pages, and the fixed texts of the caption come out in both languages. Images come out in the best quality available; if an artwork only has its thumbnail, the program tells you which one.

## Saved dossiers

Each dossier you generate is saved in the records folder, with its number and date, and never overwrites a previous one. In the selection, **Saved dossiers (n)** opens the list: for each one you can **Open**, **Save a copy…** (to take it to another folder) or **Delete**.

## Delete a selection

Open the selection and click **Edit details**: **Delete selection** asks for confirmation in a red box. The artworks are not deleted. If the selection has saved dossiers, they are also deleted and cannot be recovered: the box warns you. You confirm with **Yes, delete**.
