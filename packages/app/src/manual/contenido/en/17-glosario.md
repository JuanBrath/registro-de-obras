# Glossary

The terms the program uses, explained simply.

## Artwork

An artistic piece registered in the program: with its image, its category and its data. It can be unique or an edition.

## Unique artwork and edition artwork

A **unique artwork** is one of a kind and has a single copy. An **edition artwork** has several numbered copies: an edition.

## Edition

The numbered set of copies of an edition artwork. "Edition of 10" means copies 1/10 to 10/10.

## Copy and series

Each numbered physical unit of an artwork. The program calls them **copies** and, sometimes, **series** ("available series", "copy number"). It's the same thing.

## Artist's proof (AP)

Additional copies set aside for the artist, outside the commercial numbering. They are numbered **AP 1/2**, **AP 2/2**. The usual rule is 10% of the edition, rounded up (for 7 pieces, 1 AP; for 25 pieces, 3 AP). By convention they are not sold: they are kept, donated or used for promotion. The program still lets you record their sale.

## Status of a copy

The situation of the copy: available (the number is free but it hasn't been printed yet), in stock (printed and ready to sell), in production, on display, on consignment, reserved, sold, artist's collection, discarded or destroyed. See [Copies and statuses](cap:copias).

## Consignment

The copy is in the hands of a third party (a gallery, a fair, a warehouse) for an eventual sale, but hasn't been sold yet.

## Reservation and deposit

A **reservation** sets a copy aside for a buyer. The **deposit** is the money handed over as a down payment to hold the reservation.

## Donation

A copy handed over without a price. It has no commercial value.

## Certificate of authenticity (COA)

The document that vouches that a copy is authentic. In the program it is prepared from the copy's **Certificate** button. COA is the abbreviation of Certificate of Authenticity. See [Certificate of authenticity](cap:certificado).

## Certificate number

The consecutive number the program assigns by itself to each sale. It is used as the receipt number.

## Data sheet

A document that gathers the data of something: of an artwork, a client, the artist or the owner. It's also a certificate design in a grid format.

## Rigorous record

The part of an artwork's form with the technical data specific to its category and subtype (for example, the chemical process of an analog photograph).

## Tag

A word you put on an artwork to classify it and search for it inside the program. It isn't written into the original file. When filtering by several tags, the artworks that have at least one of them are shown. See [Filtering by tags](cap:obras).

## Synthography

Photography generated entirely by artificial intelligence. It has no camera data, and its certificate carries the legend **NO COPYRIGHT** at the bottom.

## SKU or inventory code

An identifier of your own for each artwork, to sort and search for them.

## EXIF and keywords

Data that some photos carry saved inside the file: the camera, the ISO, the capture date, keywords and rating. The program reads them when you indicate the **Original file location**.

## Fine Art

High-quality printing on conservation-grade papers, intended for collectible work.

## Commission

The percentage a gallery keeps of the sale value. It exists only in Galeris Space.

## Droit de suite (resale royalty right)

A percentage of the resale that belongs to the artist or their heirs. Each country has its own legislation. It exists only in Galeris Space, and the values are entered by hand.

## Nail to nail

A type of insurance that covers the artwork from the moment it leaves one place until it arrives at another, without gaps.

## Personal records, Galeris Studio and Galeris Space

**Galeris Studio** is the edition for an individual artist or photographer (the "personal records"). **Galeris Space** is the edition for galleries. Each one has its own records.
