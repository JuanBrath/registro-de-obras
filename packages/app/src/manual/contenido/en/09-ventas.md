# Sales, reservations and donations

How to record, edit, confirm and cancel a sale, a reservation or a donation of a copy.

## Recording a sale, reservation or donation

1. Open the artwork and find the copy. See [Copies and statuses](cap:copias).
2. Click **Sale / Reservation** (or **Sale / Reservation / Donation** on an artist's proof). A panel opens on the right.
3. Choose the **Type**: **Sale**, **Reservation** or **Donation**.
4. Fill in the details (below).
5. Click the confirm button (**Confirm sale**, **Confirm reservation** or **Confirm donation**).

The copy becomes **Sold** or **Reserved**. A donation also leaves the copy in the **Sold** status (it's no longer available), but in the copy's summary it appears as **Donated**.

If you want to leave without saving, click **Back**: the program warns "You have unsaved changes" and lets you choose **Leave without saving** or **Keep editing**.

## The buyer

- **Link to an already registered client (optional)**: pick someone from the list and their details fill in on their own.
- If you don't pick anyone, type the **Buyer's name** (required), **Email** and **Phone**. When you save, a new client is created automatically with those details, so it shows up in the statistics and in the client reports.

## Delivery and confidentiality

- **Delivery**: **Delivery address**, **City** and **Country**. If you chose a client, they are filled in with their address, but you can change them (for example, if it's shipped to a warehouse or to a third party).
- **Confidential sale**: an informative mark that the price or the buyer must not be disclosed. It doesn't restrict access inside the program.
- **Resale clause / right of first refusal**: free text.

## Date, place and advisor

- **Sale date** (or reservation / donation date): required.
- **Sale location**: the city, fair, gallery or platform where it was closed.
- **Sales advisor**: who handled the operation (it's just a name, it isn't tied to a user). It doesn't appear on donations.

## Value and currency

- **Currency**: **ARS**, **USD** or **EUR**.
- **Sale amount** (or **Reservation amount**).
- On a **reservation**, also the **Deposit amount** and the **Deposit currency**: what was handed over as a down payment to hold the reservation.
- **Donations** have no commercial value: no price is recorded.

Also, if it isn't a donation:

- **List price**, **Discount reason / authorization** and **Exchange rate applied**.
- **Costs associated with the sale**: **Framing**, **Pedestal / plinth**, **Professional packing (crate)**, **Transport / freight** and **Insurance (nail to nail)**.
- **Payment condition**: **Payment status** (**Paid**, **Pending** or **Installments / leasing**), **Payment method** and **Effective collection date**.

In Galeris Studio the artist receives the full amount: there is no commission. Commissions, VAT and other commercial data exist in Galeris Space. See [Galeris Space](cap:galeris-space).

## The certificate number

Each **sale** automatically receives a consecutive **certificate number** (1, 2, 3…). You see it when editing the sale, in the client's history and as the number of the **Sale receipt**. Reservations and donations don't get a number.

## Editing a sale

1. On the copy, click **Edit sale** (or **Edit reservation** / **Edit donation**).
2. Change what's needed and save.

The certificate number can't be changed.

## Confirming a reservation

When the reservation turns into a sale, click **Confirm sale** on the reserved copy's row. The reservation becomes a sale, the copy becomes **Sold**, it is given its **certificate number** and the reservation is noted as **fulfilled** (this is counted in the [Sales report](cap:reporte-ventas)).

## Cancelling a sale, reservation or donation

1. Click **Cancel sale** (or **Cancel reservation** / **Cancel donation**).
2. The program asks "Are you sure you want to cancel the sale?" and "What state should the copy go back to?": **Available**, **In stock**, **On display** or **Consignment**.
3. Confirm with **Yes, cancel it**.

The copy goes back to the status you chose and is free for another operation.

> Cancelling **deletes** the sale, reservation or donation from the records: it no longer appears in the client's history or in the sales report. The certificate number that a cancelled sale had stays unused (it is not assigned again). If you cancel a **reservation**, the program counts it as a **dropped** reservation in the Sales report.

## Viewing sales as a whole

For a balance by dates, artists or techniques, use the [Sales report](cap:reporte-ventas). To see everything a person has bought, use the client's sheet: [Clients](cap:clientes).
