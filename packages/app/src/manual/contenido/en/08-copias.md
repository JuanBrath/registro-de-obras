# Copies and statuses

The numbered copies of each artwork (editions and artist's proofs): their statuses, how to edit them and what can be done with each one.

## What a copy is

Each artwork has one or more copies, which appear in the program as **Copies** (and sometimes as "series"). They are the same thing: each numbered physical unit of the artwork.

- A **unique artwork** has a single copy.
- An **edition artwork** has as many copies as editions: number **1/10**, **2/10**… up to **10/10**.
- **Artist's proofs** (**AP**) are additional copies that sit outside the commercial numbering: **AP 1/2**, **AP 2/2**. They appear in a separate list, **Artist's proofs**.

By convention, artist's proofs are **not sold**: they are kept, donated or used for promotion. Even so, the program lets you record their sale if you decide to sell them; that's your decision. That's why, on an artist's proof, the button is called **Sale / Reservation / Donation**.

Each copy has its own status, its own printing data and, if it's sold, its own sale. Everything that happens to a copy (selling, reserving, donating, certifying) is done from there.

## The list of copies on the sheet

When you open an artwork, the **Copies** list first shows the copies that are **not** available (sold, reserved, etc.), which is usually what matters. If they are all available, it shows only the first one. To see the rest click **Show the other copies (n)**, and to fold the list again, **Show less**. Artist's proofs have their own button to unfold.

Each copy shows its number, its status, its data (print date, medium, location…) and, if it has a sale, a summary: "Sold to Ana on 24/09/2026 — ARS 150000 — certificate #12".

## The buttons on each copy

- **Edit**: change the copy's data.
- **Sale / Reservation** (on artist's proofs, **Sale / Reservation / Donation**): record a sale. See [Sales, reservations and donations](cap:ventas).
- If the copy already has a sale: **Edit sale** (or **Edit reservation** / **Edit donation**), **Confirm sale** (only on reservations) and **Cancel sale** (or reservation / donation).
- **Reports**: quote, receipt, delivery note and contracts. See [Reports and PDF documents](cap:informes).
- **Certificate**: the certificate of authenticity for that copy (only if it has a sale, reservation or donation). See [Certificate of authenticity](cap:certificado).

## The statuses of a copy

- **Available**: the copy number is free, but that copy hasn't been printed yet.
- **In stock**: the copy is already printed and ready to sell.
- **In production**: it's still being printed or produced, it isn't finished.
- **On display**: the copy is shown in an exhibition or show. You can set a **Deadline**.
- **Consignment**: it's in the hands of a third party (gallery, fair, warehouse) for an eventual sale, but hasn't been sold yet. It also accepts a **Deadline**.
- **Reserved**: it has a recorded reservation.
- **Sold**: it has a recorded sale.
- **Artist's collection**: it stayed with the artist and is not available for sale or reservation.
- **Discarded**: it was discarded because of a printing defect or another reason, without becoming a valid piece.
- **Destroyed**: the copy was destroyed and no longer physically exists.

The **Reserved** and **Sold** statuses are not chosen by hand: they appear on their own when you record a reservation or a sale. While a copy has a sale, reservation or donation, its status can't be changed from the edit form either: to change it you have to modify or cancel that sale.

## Editing a copy

1. On the copy, click **Edit**. The copy's form opens.
2. Fill in or correct the data. Depending on the artwork's category, some fields or others appear; the main ones are:
   - **Status** (and **Deadline** if it's display or consignment).
   - **Print date** and **Medium** (the paper or other support).
   - **Process/print type** (in digital photography and graphic work) and **Ink type** (in digital photography, synthography and graphic work).
   - **Print workshop / Lab** (in digital photography, graphic work and sculpture).
   - **Size (mm)**, **Full sheet / support size** and **Weight**.
   - **Mounting / Conservation**, **Final size framed (mm)**, **Glass / Front protection** and **Hanging system** (in the categories that get framed: photography, painting, graphic work and drawing).
   - **Signature location** and **Dry seal / Holograms** (where they apply).
   - **Current location of this copy** and **Value** (the copy's price).
   - **Notes**.
   - In graphic work and sculpture, artist's proofs also have a **Special proof classification** (P/E, B.A.T., H/C, P/I, F/C).
3. Click **Save changes**.

The **?** tips explain each item. None of them is required, except what's needed to sell (see below).

> In Galeris Space, the copy also has gallery-specific data: the **Certificate of Authenticity (COA) number**, the **COA issuer**, the **COA issue date**, the **COA security system**, the **Insurance value (nail-to-nail)** and the **Condition report**.

## What is needed before selling or reserving

To record a sale or a reservation, the copy must have its **print date** and its **print medium** filled in. If either is missing, when you click the button the program tells you what's missing ("You can't register a sale or reservation for 3/10: missing the print date") and sends you to complete it with **Edit**.

Sales can't be recorded for copies that are **Discarded**, **Destroyed** or in the **Artist's collection**.
