# Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to the most common questions.

## I can't record a sale

Check these causes:

- The copy is missing the **print date** or the **print medium**. The program tells you what's missing: fill it in with **Edit**. See [Copies and statuses](cap:copias).
- The copy is **Discarded**, **Destroyed** or in the **Artist's collection**: it can't be sold.
- The copy already has a sale, reservation or donation: in that case you see **Edit sale** instead of **Sale / Reservation**.

## Can I sell an artist's proof?

The program lets you: on an artist's proof the button is called **Sale / Reservation / Donation**. By convention, artist's proofs are not sold (they are kept, donated or used for promotion), but if you decide to sell one, it is recorded like any other sale. See [Copies and statuses](cap:copias).

## I chose several tags and I see artworks that don't have all of them

That's expected: with several tags, the filter shows the artworks that have **at least one** of them. Each tag you add widens what you see. See [Artworks](cap:obras).

## The certificate says NO COPYRIGHT

This happens with artworks of the **Synthography** subtype (art generated by artificial intelligence): instead of the artist's copyright, the certificate states that there isn't one. See [Certificate of authenticity](cap:certificado).

## I can't change the status of a copy

If the copy has a sale, reservation or donation, the status is managed from there: modify or cancel that operation. **Sold** and **Reserved** are not chosen by hand. See [Sales, reservations and donations](cap:ventas).

## I can't delete an artwork, an artist or a client

If they have recorded sales (or, in the case of an artist, artworks), the program doesn't delete them so that history isn't lost. First you have to deal with those sales or artworks.

## I can't turn my unique artwork into an edition (or the other way around), or lower the number of editions

This is only possible if the copies have no history of their own. If any of them has a status or loaded data, the program tells you which ones and doesn't allow it. See [Artwork sheet](cap:ficha-de-obra).

## My artworks disappeared from the screen

The program may be pointing to another data folder, or an external drive may not be connected. See [Getting started](cap:primeros-pasos) and [Settings](cap:configuracion).

## macOS won't let me open the program

Since the program isn't yet signed with an Apple Developer account, macOS may block the first opening. Right-click (or Control + click) the program and choose **Open**.

## The image of my PSD doesn't show

A PSD or PSB can only be used if it has a preview saved inside it. Open it in Photoshop, turn on **Image Previews** in the preferences and save it again, or use a JPG or PNG. See [Adding a new artwork](cap:nueva-obra).

## The certificate or the report comes out without a signature or without a logo

You have to save them first in **My profile (owner)**: your logo and your digital signature. Then, in the report, choose **With logo** and **Digital signature** (in the certificate, tick **Include the logo** and **Include the digital signature**). See [My profile](cap:mi-perfil).

## A certificate field is grey and I can't type in it

That data comes from your records. You can correct it with **Correct records** or edit it in the artwork, the copy or the sale. See [Certificate of authenticity](cap:certificado).

## Where is my data and how do I make a backup?

It's in the data folder you chose the first time. To make a backup, copy that whole folder to another place (for example, an external drive). See [Settings](cap:configuracion) to learn how to change or move it.

## Can I sync my data with iCloud, Dropbox or Google Drive?

It's not advisable: those services can damage the database file if they sync it while the program is writing. Use a local folder and make manual copies.
