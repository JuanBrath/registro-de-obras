# Differences with Galeris Space (gallery)

Galeris Space is the version designed for galleries. It shares almost everything with Galeris Studio; this chapter lists what changes.

## What Galeris Space is

If your installation includes Galeris Space, on the welcome screen you see the **Galeris Space** button as well as **Galeris Studio**. Each one has its own records, with its own data. An installation with both editions (Galeris Suite) lets you switch from one to the other from the home screen.

## Galeris Space home buttons

- **My Gallery**: the gallery's details (name, logo, digital signature, contact). It replaces **My profile (owner)**.
- **Artists**: the artists the gallery represents. Each artwork belongs to an artist.
- **Artworks**, **Clients** and **Sales**: the same as in Studio.

## Artists

In **Artists** you add each artist with their artist number (assigned by the program on its own), photo, contact details, biography, artist statement and résumé (education, exhibitions, awards, collections, publications). You search by name and you can produce the **Artist sheet (PDF)**. An artist with registered artworks can't be deleted, and if you add one with a name that already exists, the program warns you and lets you **Add anyway**.

When adding an artwork you choose the **Artist** from a list (or add a new one right there with **+ New artist**).

## When adding artworks

- The **Acquisition method** (direct consignment from the studio, deposit from a private collection, outright purchase by the gallery) and the **Provenance and exhibition history** are added.
- The artwork search box also searches by artist and by tag, and there is a filter by artist.

## On each copy

The gallery data is added: the **Certificate of Authenticity (COA) number**, the **COA issuer**, the **COA issue date**, the **COA security system**, the **Insurance value (nail-to-nail)** and the **Condition report**.

## In sales

The whole commercial block for galleries is added:

- **Gallery commission applies**, with **Commission percentage**, **Commission amount** and **Net amount for the artist**, and the **Artist settlement status** (**Pending settlement**, **Settled**, **Payment receipt issued**).
- **VAT percentage** and **VAT amount**, **Withholdings** and **Customs duties (export)**.
- **Resale royalty applies** (droit de suite), with its percentage and amount.

In Studio, on the other hand, the artist receives the full amount and those fields don't appear.

The **Sales report** also totals how much is owed to each artist (net commission plus droit de suite) and lets you filter by settlement status. See [Sales report](cap:reporte-ventas).

## In certificates

Besides **Classic**, **Simple** and **Data sheet, no gallery signature**, Galeris Space offers the **Data sheet** design, with the signature line of the gallery owner and the gallery's contact details at the bottom. See [Certificate of authenticity](cap:certificado).
