# Getting started

What Galeris Studio is and how to get it ready the first time you open it.

## What is Galeris Studio

Galeris Studio is the program where you keep the record of your work: each artwork with its photo and technical details, the numbered copies of each edition, sales, reservations and donations, your clients, and the documents generated from all of that (data sheets, quotes, receipts, certificates of authenticity, contracts).

Everything you enter is saved on your computer, in a folder you choose. You don't need an internet connection to use it.

## Opening the program for the first time

1. Open Galeris. You'll see the welcome screen, with the logo and the **Galeris Studio** button.
2. Click **Galeris Studio**.
3. The first time, the program asks you to choose a folder where all your data will be saved. Pick a folder on your computer (there are recommendations below).
4. Then it asks you to fill in **My info** (your profile as the owner). This step is required: you can't continue until you save your details. See [My profile](cap:mi-perfil).
5. When you finish, you reach the home screen, with the buttons to start working. See [Home screen and navigation](cap:inicio).

## Which folder to choose

In that folder Galeris saves the database with all your information, the photos of your artworks and the files it generates.

- Choose a folder **on your computer or on an external drive**.
- **Don't choose a folder inside iCloud Drive, Dropbox, Google Drive or OneDrive.** Those services sync files in the background, and if they do it while Galeris is writing, the database can be damaged and information lost. If you pick a folder of that kind, the program warns you and lets you pick another one.
- Write down where the folder is: it's the most valuable thing you have in the program.

> Tip: make a copy of that folder from time to time (for example, on an external drive). The program doesn't make backups on its own.

## If the program can't find the folder

This can happen if you saved your data on an external drive that isn't connected, or if you moved or renamed the folder. When you open your records, the notice **Data folder not found** appears with two options:

- **Find it**: you choose again the folder where your data is. This is the right choice in almost every case.
- **Create a new folder**: starts an empty record. Your previous data is not deleted, but the program stops showing it until you point it back to that folder (done from **Settings**, with **Change folder…**). It asks you to confirm before doing this.

## Changing or moving the folder later

This is done from the settings. See [Settings](cap:configuracion).

## Closing your records and going back to the welcome screen

Click the **Galeris** logo at the top left to go back to the welcome screen. Your data stays saved; to keep working, click **Galeris Studio** again.
