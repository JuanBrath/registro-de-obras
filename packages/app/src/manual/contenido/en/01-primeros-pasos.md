# Getting started

What Recer Studio is and how to get it ready the first time you open it.

## What Recer means

In Catalan, "recer" means shelter, refuge and protection ("estar a recer" is to be safe from the elements). Every work of art needs a space where it can be preserved, documented and valued over time. Recer was born with that purpose: to offer a solid digital infrastructure to safeguard the archive, the traceability and the history of every piece.

That is why the program's motto is **Shelter · refuge · protection**. The first time you open the program you see this explanation on the Why Recer? screen, and you can read it again by clicking the logo on the start screen. Recer comes in two editions: Recer Studio, for artists and photographers, and Recer Space, for galleries.

## What is Recer Studio

Recer Studio is the program where you keep the record of your work: each artwork with its photo and technical details, the numbered copies of each edition, sales, reservations and donations, your clients, and the documents generated from all of that (data sheets, quotes, receipts, certificates of authenticity, contracts).

Everything you enter is saved on your computer, in a folder you choose. You don't need an internet connection to use it.

## Opening the program for the first time

1. Open Recer. The first time you see a screen that explains **Why Recer?**: read it and click **Get started**. Then you'll see the welcome screen, with the logo and the **Recer Studio** button. To read the explanation again, click the Recer logo on that screen.
2. Click **Recer Studio**.
3. The first time, the program asks you to choose a folder where all your data will be saved. Pick a folder on your computer (there are recommendations below).
4. Then it asks you to fill in **My info** (your profile as the owner). This step is required: you can't continue until you save your details. See [My profile](cap:mi-perfil).
5. When you finish, you reach the home screen, with the buttons to start working. See [Home screen and navigation](cap:inicio).

## Which folder to choose

In that folder Recer saves the database with all your information, the photos of your artworks and the files it generates.

- Choose a folder **on your computer or on an external drive**.
- **Don't choose a folder inside iCloud Drive, Dropbox, Google Drive or OneDrive.** Those services sync files in the background, and if they do it while Recer is writing, the database can be damaged and information lost. If you pick a folder of that kind, the program warns you and lets you pick another one.
- Write down where the folder is: it's the most valuable thing you have in the program.

> Tip: make a copy of that folder from time to time (for example, on an external drive). The program doesn't make backups on its own.

## If the program can't find the folder

This can happen if you saved your data on an external drive that isn't connected, or if you moved or renamed the folder. When you open your records, the notice **Data folder not found** appears with **Find it** or **Cancel**:

- **Find it**: you choose again the folder where your data is. This is the right choice in almost every case.
- **Cancel**: asks whether you want to **create a new folder** instead (starts an empty record; your previous data isn't deleted, but the program stops showing it until you point it back to that folder, from **Settings**, with **Change folder…**) — and there you can also **cancel** and go back to the previous screen without changing anything.

## Changing or moving the folder later

This is done from the settings. See [Settings](cap:configuracion).

## Closing your records and going back to the welcome screen

Click the **Recer** logo at the top left to go back to the welcome screen. Your data stays saved; to keep working, click **Recer Studio** again.
