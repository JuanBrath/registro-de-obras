# My profile

Your details as the owner of the records: you fill them in once and they are used automatically in every new artwork and in the documents.

## What it's for

The details in **My info** identify the author of the artworks in these records. In addition, your **logo** and your **digital signature** are used in the reports and certificates you generate.

To open it, on the home screen click **My profile (owner)**. The first time you open the program this screen appears by itself and you have to fill it in to continue. See [Getting started](cap:primeros-pasos).

## Filling in your details

1. **Photo**: your photo as the owner (optional). Click it to see it enlarged.
2. **Personal logo**: your logo (optional). It appears in reports if you choose **With logo**.
3. **Digital signature**: an image of your signature, with a transparent or white background. It is inserted automatically in the reports where you choose **Digital signature**, instead of leaving a blank space to sign by hand.
4. **Full name**: this is the only required field.
5. **Date of birth**, **Short Bio**, **Biography (English)** and **Notes**: optional. If you generate a report in English and the English biography is empty, the Spanish one is used as it is.
6. In the **Contact** group: **Email**, **Phone**, **Address**, **City/Locality**, **CUIT**, **Website**, **Instagram**, **Facebook** and **X (Twitter)**. The **City/Locality** is used, for example, next to the date in the header of reports.
7. Click **Save my info**. The notice "Info saved successfully." appears.

> If there are unsaved changes, the program won't let you go back: it tells you to save them with **Save my info** before leaving.

## Changing the signature or the logo

Click the image or the button to choose a new file and then **Save my info**. For the signature to look good in the documents, an image with a transparent background (PNG) works best.

## Generating the owner's data sheet as a PDF

1. Click **Generate report**.
2. Choose **Full sheet (all data on file)** or **Biography only (personal data and biography)**.
3. Choose the language, whether it includes the logo and whether it includes the signature. See [Reports and PDF documents](cap:informes).
4. Click **Generate** and choose where to save the file.
