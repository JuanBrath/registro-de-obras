# Images view (artwork gallery)

A view of your artworks as large photos, to look at them, rate them and quickly check their availability.

## Opening the Images view

In **Artworks**, next to **Columns** and **Cards**, click **Images**. It is the same screen and the same filters you had on; only how the artworks are shown changes: just the photos, without text. It also exists in **Selections**, with the artworks of the open selection. Under each photo you see the artwork's name and its rating on a single line ("3 ★", or "0 ★" if it has none). To rate, click that number: the five stars unfold and a second click chooses how many (clicking the same amount again removes it). Artworks that have no photo yet do not appear in this view.

## Filtering

At the top you have the list's filters, in a bar that stays pinned while you scroll through the photos: **All categories**, subtype, star rating and tags. The filters work the same way as in the list: they combine with each other and, with several tags, the photos that have **at least one** of them are shown. See [Filtering by tags](cap:obras).

## Viewing a photo large

1. Click a thumbnail: the photo opens enlarged. In this view a click does **not** open the sheet (in Columns and Cards it does).
2. With the arrows **‹** and **›** you go to the previous or the next one. At the bottom you see the title and where you are ("3 / 25").
3. You can **rate with stars** the photo you're viewing.
4. Click the **✕** or press **Esc** to close.

## The right-click menu on the photo

Right-clicking a photo (the thumbnail in the grid or the enlarged view) opens a menu with four options, instead of the system's default menu.

### Open sheet

Opens the artwork's full sheet without having to enlarge the photo first.

### Export image…

- If the artwork doesn't have the **Original file location** loaded, the notice "Can't export: this artwork doesn't have the original file loaded (see "Original file location" in Edit artwork)." appears and nothing opens.
- If it does, a window opens where you choose the **Format** (**JPG**, **TIFF** or **Original**), and for JPG or TIFF the **Reference side**, the **Size (in pixels)** and an optional **Copyright**. See the full details in [Artwork sheet](cap:ficha-de-obra).

### Open in Photoshop

Opens the original file in Adobe Photoshop (the newest copy, if there are several versions installed). If the artwork has no original file loaded or there's no copy of Photoshop installed, the corresponding notice appears — see [Artwork sheet](cap:ficha-de-obra).

### Open in Lightroom

Brings Lightroom Classic to the front and, if the plugin is installed, finds and selects that photo in the open catalog. If the artwork has no original file loaded, the corresponding notice appears — see [Artwork sheet](cap:ficha-de-obra) and [Lightroom Classic](cap:lightroom-classic).

## Viewing the artwork's information

With the photo open, click the **ⓘ** button (**View artwork info**). A small window opens with:

- The category and whether it's a unique artwork or an edition.
- The availability ("3/10 available") and how many copies there are in each status.
- If the artwork is an edition, the **First available copy**: its **Copy number**, **Print date**, **Medium**, **Size (mm)** and **Current location of this copy**. If none is left, it says "There is no available copy right now."

It's a quick look-up. If you need more (to edit it, generate reports, record a sale), click **View this artwork**: its full sheet opens. To go back, use the **✕** or the button at the bottom. See [Artwork sheet](cap:ficha-de-obra).
