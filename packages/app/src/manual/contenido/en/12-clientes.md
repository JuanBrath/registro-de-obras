# Clients

Your buyers and contacts: how to add them, search for them, see everything each one has bought and produce sheets and reports.

## Opening the screen

On the home screen, click **Clients**. You see the **Registered clients**. If there aren't any yet, "No clients registered yet." appears.

## Adding a new client

1. Click **New client**.
2. Fill in the details:
   - **Name or Business name**: required.
   - **Client type**: **Private collector**, **Gallery/Dealer**, **Company/Institution** or **Decorator/Architect**.
   - **Address**, **City**, **Country**.
   - **Email** and **Phone**.
   - **CUIT**.
   - **Interest profile**: favorite themes, techniques or formats (for example, "prefers natural wood frames").
   - **Notes**.
3. Click **Add client**. To leave without saving, **Cancel**.

> Clients are also created automatically: when you record a sale and type a new buyer without choosing one from the list, the program creates the client with those details. See [Sales, reservations and donations](cap:ventas).

## Searching for a client

The search box finds matches in any of the client's data, not just the name: it's enough to type part of a phone number, an email or a code.

## Viewing, editing or deleting

Each client in the list has three buttons:

- **View**: opens their sheet to read it.
- **Edit**: opens their sheet to modify it. Save with **Save** or leave with **Cancel**.
- **Delete**: asks for confirmation ("Delete this client? This cannot be undone."). If the client has recorded sales it can't be deleted.

## The client's sheet

It has two tabs:

- **Client data**: all their details.
- **History / Collection Acquired**: everything they bought, with **Date**, **Artwork**, **Edition**, **Format / Support**, **Final amount** and **Certificate Status (COA)** (**Issued (#n)** if the sale has a certificate number, or **Pending**).

## Reports for a client

Inside the sheet, click **Generate report** and choose:

- **Sheet with current data**.
- **Blank sheet to complete (internal use)**.
- **Blank sheet for the client to complete**.
- **Purchase history by date**: asks for a **From** / **To** range.

## Reports for the whole list

Above the list, **Generate reports** offers:

- **Blank sheet for the client to complete**.
- **General client list**: name, type, phone, email, city and country.
- **Sheet of clients matching the search**: generates one sheet for each client that appears with the current search (it tells you how many will be generated).

For language, logo and signature, see [Reports and PDF documents](cap:informes).
