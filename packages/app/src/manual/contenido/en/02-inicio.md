# Home screen and navigation

How to get around the program: the home buttons, the gear, the back buttons and the help on each field.

## The home screen

After opening your records you see the name **Galeris Studio**, your name as the owner, and these buttons:

- **My profile (owner)**: your details, your logo and your signature. See [My profile](cap:mi-perfil).
- **Artworks**: the list of all your artworks. From there you add new artworks. See [Artworks](cap:obras).
- **Clients**: your buyers and contacts. See [Clients](cap:clientes).
- **Sales**: the sales report by dates. See [Sales report](cap:reporte-ventas).
- **User manual**: this manual.

Below them, some thumbnails of your artworks may appear, picked at random, as decoration.

## The top bar

- The **Galeris** logo (top left) closes your records and takes you back to the welcome screen.
- The gear ⚙ (top right) opens **Settings**. See [Settings](cap:configuracion).

## Going back

On almost every screen there are two ways to go back:

- The **✕** at the top right.
- The **Back** button at the bottom of the screen.

Both do the same thing. If you're entering data and you leave without saving, some screens warn you that the changes will be lost and let you choose between **Leave without saving** and **Keep editing**.

## Help on each field

Many fields have a small help icon **?** next to them. Click it and a tip appears with a short explanation of what goes in that field and, sometimes, examples. It closes with the Esc key or by clicking elsewhere.

## Enter and Tab in forms

When you're filling in a form, the **Enter** key doesn't submit it: it moves to the next field, just like the Tab key. To save you have to click the save button. (In multi-line text boxes, Enter starts a new line.)

## Windows and notices

- Pop-up windows close with their **✕**, with the **Back** button or with the **Esc** key.
- Error notices appear in red with the ⚠️ symbol, and success notices in green with ✅.

## Searching the manual and printing it

When you open the manual (**User manual** button) you see the **Contents** of chapters on the left and, on the right, the chapter you're reading. To jump to another chapter click it in the contents, or use the **← Previous chapter** and **Next chapter →** buttons at the end of each chapter.

- **Search**: type what you need in the box at the top, in your own words, for example "how do I change the data folder" or "digital signature". The manual shows the sections that talk about it, with the words highlighted; clicking a result opens that section. Capital letters and plurals don't matter.
- **Generate PDF**: the **Generate PDF** button (next to the search box) builds a file to print or save. You can choose the **Complete manual**, which has a cover and a contents page with the page number of each chapter, or **Only the chapter** you have open. Then you choose where to save it. In the PDF, each reference to another chapter has its number next to it, for example "My profile (ch. 4)", since links can't be clicked on paper.
