# Reports and PDF documents

All the documents the program generates and how the language, the logo and the signature are chosen.

## Where they are generated

Almost every screen has a **Generate report** or **Reports** button:

- **Artworks** (list): summary list of artworks. See [Artworks](cap:obras).
- **An artwork's sheet**: data sheet, series detail and report for third parties. See [Artwork sheet](cap:ficha-de-obra).
- **Each copy**: quote, sale receipt, delivery note and contracts (below).
- **Certificate**: the certificate of authenticity has its own button. See [Certificate of authenticity](cap:certificado).
- **Clients**: client sheets, lists and purchase history. See [Clients](cap:clientes).
- **My profile**: the owner's data sheet. See [My profile](cap:mi-perfil).
- **Sales**: report as a PDF and as a spreadsheet. See [Sales report](cap:reporte-ventas).

## The reports window

When you click **Generate report** a window opens where you choose what to produce and how:

1. Choose the report from the list.
2. If that option has data of its own (for example, a date range), fill it in.
3. Choose the common options (below).
4. Click **Generate**.
5. The box to choose where to save the PDF opens. When it finishes, the program asks whether you want to open it to see it now, with the choices **Abrir PDF** ("Open PDF") or **Cerrar** ("Close"). This prompt is shown in Spanish.

### Common options

- **Report language**: **Español**, **English** or **Ambos / Both** (both languages in the same document). It changes the fixed titles and texts; the data comes out as you entered it. Contracts are generated in Spanish only.
- **Logo**: **With logo** or **Without logo**. With logo it uses the one you saved in your profile; if you haven't saved one, it uses the Galeris monogram.
- **Date in the letterhead**: **With date** or **Without date** (in the reports that offer it). Next to the date goes your city, if you entered it.
- **Notes** and **Statement**: **Include** or **Omit** (in an artwork's report, when it has those texts filled in).
- **Signature**: **No signature**, **Digital signature** (uses the signature image from your profile; it's disabled if you haven't saved one) or **Space for handwritten signature** (leaves a line to sign by hand).

## Reports for a copy

On each copy, the **Reports** button offers, depending on the copy's status:

- **Generate quote**: quotes that specific copy. It's only offered if the copy is not sold, reserved, discarded, destroyed or in the artist's collection.
- **Sale receipt**: the receipt for a sale or reservation, with the sale's certificate number and the image of the artwork. It's not offered for donations.
- **Delivery note**: where the artwork is delivered and in what condition it leaves.
- **Sale contract (standard)**: sales only.
- **Sale contract (with right of first refusal)**: sales only. It asks for the **Right of first refusal term (years)**, the **Term to exercise the right (days)** and the **Price-matching criterion**.

The receipt, the delivery note and the contracts use the data of the recorded sale and of the buyer (with their address and CUIT if the client has them filled in). See [Sales, reservations and donations](cap:ventas).

## Where the files are saved

You choose the folder each time, in the save box. The program suggests a name according to the document (for example, `presupuesto_Title_3_10.pdf` or `remito_Title_3_10.pdf`).
