# Sales report

The balance of your sales between two dates, with totals, summaries and the option to download it as a PDF or a spreadsheet.

## Opening the report

On the home screen, click **Sales**.

## Choosing what to see

1. **From** and **To**: the dates come pre-filled with the current month. Change them to the range you want. The "To" date can't be earlier than "From".
2. If you want, filter by **Client**, **Artist**, **Technique** or **Sales advisor**. In each one, **All** doesn't filter.
3. In Galeris Space, you can also filter by **Settlement status** (**Pending settlement**, **Settled** or **Payment receipt issued**).
4. Click **Search**.

The report counts **sales** only: it doesn't include reservations or donations. If there are none in the range, "No sales recorded in that date range." appears.

## What it shows

- A table with each sale: **Date**, **Artist**, **Artwork**, **Edition**, **Technique**, **Advisor**, **Currency**, **Sale value**, **Commission value**, **Net** and **Margin**.
- **Total**, with the **Gross**, the **Net** and the **Margin**.
- **Summary by artist** and **Summary by technique**.
- In Galeris Space, **Settlement to artists**: how much is owed to each artist (**Pending settlement**) and how much has already been paid (**Already settled**), adding up the net of the sale and the resale right (droit de suite) for sales that have it. To mark a sale as settled you still go into that specific sale, as before.
- **Average inventory age**: how many days, on average, the available or in-stock artworks have been around ("… days on average").
- **Reservations in this period**: how many were **Fulfilled** (became a sale) and how many **Dropped** (were cancelled).

## Downloading the report

Once you've searched, two buttons appear:

- **Generate PDF**: the **Sales report** for the chosen range, titled "From … to …".
- **Generate spreadsheet**: an Excel spreadsheet (.xlsx) with the same data, to work with on your own.

In both cases you choose where to save the file.
