# Certificate of authenticity

How to prepare the certificate of authenticity (COA) for a sold, reserved or donated copy, with a preview, and how to correct data from there.

## What it is and when it can be prepared

The certificate goes with every copy that leaves your studio. It is prepared from a copy that already has a **sale**, a **reservation** or a **donation** recorded, and it takes most of its data from what you already entered: you don't have to type it again.

## Opening the certificate screen

1. Open the artwork and find the copy. See [Copies and statuses](cap:copias).
2. Click the **Certificate** button (it's next to **Reports**). If it doesn't appear, the copy doesn't have a sale, reservation or donation yet.
3. A large screen opens: the data on the left and the certificate preview on the right, which updates by itself every time you change something.

## Choosing how it looks

In the **Layout** section:

- **Design**:
  - **Classic**: framed, with a large photo.
  - **Simple**: no frame, with centered text.
  - **Data sheet**: a grid with the details, with a signature line for the artist and for the gallery (only in Galeris Space).
  - **Data sheet, no gallery signature**: the same grid, with the artist's signature only.
- **Paper size**: **A4**, **Letter** or **A5**. The design is built for that size, it isn't shrunk when printing.
- **Label language**: **Español**, **English** or **Ambos / Both**. It only changes the titles of each field; the data comes out as entered.
- **Cut guides**: **None**, **Corners** (cut marks) or **Outline** (thin line). They are for printing on a larger sheet or on a roll: the PDF comes out a little bigger, with the guides outside the chosen sheet. Print it at actual size (100%) and cut along the guides.

## The greyed-out and the enabled data

In **Certificate details**:

- The data the program already has (title, artist, series, copy number, size, print, year taken, image, signature, logo…) comes out **in grey**: it comes from your records and can't be changed in that field.
- The fields that are **empty** in your records come out **enabled**: you can fill them in, but what you write is valid **only for this certificate**, it isn't saved in the artwork.

A field you don't fill in doesn't appear on the certificate.

## Synthography artworks: the NO COPYRIGHT legend

If the artwork is a **synthography** (art generated entirely by artificial intelligence), the certificate carries the legend **NO COPYRIGHT** at the bottom, in capital letters, instead of the artist's copyright. This happens in all three designs (**Classic**, **Simple** and **Data sheet**) and you don't have to do anything: the program adds it by itself according to the artwork's subtype. On the certificate screen a notice appears saying so.

- In the **Classic** certificate, the legend replaces the line "© Artist - All rights reserved".
- Depending on the **Label language**, it reads **SIN COPYRIGHT**, **NO COPYRIGHT** or both: **SIN COPYRIGHT · NO COPYRIGHT**.
- Other artworks keep carrying the artist's copyright in the Classic design.

## Artist's proofs and the copy number

The copy number comes out as **3/10**. If the artwork has artist's proofs, the certificate adds the number: **3/10 + 2 AP**. If the copy is itself an artist's proof (**AP 1/2**), nothing more is added.

## Signature and logo

- **Include the digital signature**: tick it for the signature saved in your profile to appear on the certificate. If it's not ticked, the line to sign by hand is left. If you haven't saved a signature, you can choose an image for this certificate.
- **Include the logo**: the same, with the logo from your profile. If there is no logo, the certificate comes out without one. The **Simple** design has no logo.

## Issue

- **Place** and **Date**: they come from the sale (the sale's place and date) and come out in grey.
- If the sale has no place, the field stays enabled so you can type it.

## Correcting a wrong detail

If you see something wrong, there are two ways:

### Edit in the artwork, the copy or the sale

The **Edit the artwork**, **Edit the copy** and **Edit the sale** buttons close the certificate and open the corresponding form. This is the way for any data.

### Correct records (without leaving the screen)

1. Click **Correct records**.
2. A notice appears: the changes are **not only for this certificate**, they are also saved in the artwork, the copy or the sale where each item was entered, and they will be seen throughout the program and in future reports. If you agree, click **Got it, I want to correct details**.
3. The details that can be corrected are enabled, with a gold border. Each one says where it is saved: **Saved in the artwork**, **Saved in the copy** or **Saved in the sale**.
4. Change what you need. At the top a box lists what will be updated ("Will be updated: Title (artwork), Image size (copy)").
5. Click **Save changes to your records**. "Records updated." appears and the fields go back to grey with the corrected data. If you prefer not to save, **Discard changes**.

You can correct these from here:

- In the **artwork**: **Title**, **Series**, **Year taken** (on the data sheet, it's the artwork's year if it has one) and **Edition year** (shown in the Classic design).
- In the **copy**: **Image size**, **Process/print type**, **Medium** and **Signature placement**.
- In the **sale**: **Place** and **Date**.

Everything is saved together or nothing is saved. If something isn't valid, the program says so and doesn't save:

- The **title** can't be left empty.
- The **year taken** and the **edition year** must be 4 digits (the edition year can be left empty).
- The **date** must be a real date.

The rest (copy number, artist's proofs, subtype, artist, gallery data and images) stays grey and is changed with the **Edit…** buttons. While you are correcting, those buttons are locked so you don't lose unsaved changes.

> If you close the screen with unsaved changes to your records, the program asks what to do: **Save and leave**, **Discard and leave** or **Keep editing**.

## Saving the PDF

Click **Save PDF**, choose where to save it and, if you want to see it right away, **Open PDF**. The file is named, for example, `coa_The_Sky_in_the_Water_3_10.pdf`.

## Frequently asked questions

- **Why doesn't the Certificate button appear?** Because the copy doesn't have a sale, reservation or donation yet.
- **I typed something in an enabled field and then it isn't in the artwork.** That's expected: what you fill in an enabled field is valid only for that certificate. For it to stay in your records, enter it in the artwork, the copy or the sale.
